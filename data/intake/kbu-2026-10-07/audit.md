The register contains **120 groups**: **106 supported**, **10 evidence only**, **2 assertion**, **2 overstated**. Repeated recommendations and inventory metadata are grouped. Design-dependent numbers and closing recommendations are traced in the fix column. Version identifiers are not measurements; verdict counts are not a quality percentage.

**Supported** means evidence and reasoning warrant the scope. **Evidence only** means evidence exists but the bridge to the exact claim is missing. **Assertion** means supporting evidence was unavailable. **Overstated** means scope/certainty strengthened or a number misread. A supported proposal need not be implemented or approved. A proposed number still needs justification. Unavailable attribution stays open; it does not establish falsity.

Quotations were compared at their stated lines, retaining digits, operators and hedges. [quote-checks.md](/Users/GaryT/Documents/Work/tools/kbu-2026-10-07/A1/quote-checks.md) supplies the CERIC checker; [verification.json](/Users/GaryT/Documents/Work/tools/kbu-2026-10-07/A1/verification.json) records exact-text/hash checks. A separate reviewer caught the GPU overstatement and challenged false contradictions about staged support and hypothetical hosting. Coordinator acceptance of revised D1 remains pending.

Terms: **KB** means knowledge base, the searchable collection; a **graph** stores typed relationships. An **embedding** represents content as numbers. A **harness** runs an agent; **MCP**, Model Context Protocol, connects tools; **CLI** is a command-line interface. A **promotion gate** sets activation checks. **NDCG@10** rewards relevant results ranked higher; this KB’s **recall@10** counts queries with a positive result in ten. **p95** is the 95th percentile. **Float32/float16/int8** identify precision. **BM25** ranks keywords; **DBSF** combines keyword/vector scores. A **tokenizer** counts text units (**tokens**); a **reranker** rescores results. **JSON** is structured data; **YAML frontmatter** is a note’s metadata header. A **generation** is a published snapshot; **Matryoshka** embeddings permit smaller coordinate prefixes. **CPU/GPU** mean central/graphics processing unit. Quoted source/tool names remain identifiers.

Measurements were not rerun. I checked arithmetic, populations, machines, samples and exclusions. M3 retains same-agent review and shared-server timing; M2 retains page-access rather than answer scores. External checks are marked † and linked; dates are below. No models, benchmarks, GPU jobs, service changes or repository writes occurred.
