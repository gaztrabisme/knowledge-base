# Chinese common-character sets

- `chinese_common_hans.txt`: the 3,500-character Level 1 list of the 2013 Table of General Standard Chinese Characters, downloaded from [shengdoushi/common-standard-chinese-characters-table](https://github.com/shengdoushi/common-standard-chinese-characters-table).
- `chinese_common_hant.txt`: the union of those Level 1 characters and every single-character Traditional mapping supplied by OpenCC `STCharacters.txt` (Apache-2.0, [source](https://github.com/BYVoid/OpenCC/blob/master/data/dictionary/STCharacters.txt)); ambiguous mappings are retained.

Vendored list counts: Hans 3500; Hant 4928.
