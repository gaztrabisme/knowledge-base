**Grade 1 does not have the same consequence for an original label and a proposed addition.** Adding requires grade 2; removing a proposed wrong label requires grade 0. Original positive controls are calibration inputs, not removal candidates. Consequently, retaining an original label does not mean that a verifying judge gave it grade 2.
