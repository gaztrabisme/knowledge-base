# PMBOK v9 sanity miss diagnosis

Branch: query-or-label

## Finding

The labeled chunk is present and its text is intact. The miss is caused by the sanity query’s indirect wording: it paraphrases the airport example but omits the passage’s strongest retrieval terms. PMBOK itself is indexed and answers ordinary PMBOK questions in the top 10. An exact-anchor control query retrieves the labeled passage at rank 1.

## Evidence

**Sanity row 1 and reproduction.** The query is: “For a planned airport with uncertain retail demand, how could the team estimate likely customer behavior before those facilities exist?” Running the requested v9 `test_search.py` command with `--top-k 50 --no-rerank --json` returned 42 parent-deduplicated results and no PMBOK passage. The fused window contains no PMBOK point.

**Labeled chunk.** `pmbok-guide-7e-p46-c7` has 1,242 characters and 188 whitespace-delimited words. It contains the complete `Simulation` bullet, including the sentence about using shopping malls and entertainment establishments to learn consumer buying habits for a new airport. The chunk also contains the preceding `Decoupling` bullet and following `Reframing` material; it is not a cut-off fragment. Its `heading_path` is `A Guide to the Project Management Body of Knowledge (PMBOK® Guide) > 2. Project Performance Domains > 2.8. Uncertainty Performance Domain (part 1) (part 1)`. The repeated suffix is visibly wrong. The source heading at `data/markdown/pmbok-guide-7e.md:2118` is clean, and the heading map has the clean level-3 entry `2.8. Uncertainty Performance Domain`. The suffix is generated by chunk splitting and overlap in `knowledge_base/chunk.py`. It is a metadata defect, but does not explain this retrieval miss: the unchanged indexed point is rank 1 when queried with its distinctive content terms (see below).

**Index integrity.** `developer_knowledge_v9` has 286,723 points, including 523 PMBOK points for the 523 children in `data/chunks/pmbok-guide-7e.json`. The PMBOK points have 523 unique child IDs. I compared every point’s `child_text` and `heading_path` to its chunk record: 0 mismatches and 0 missing IDs. In particular, the server payload for `pmbok-guide-7e-p46-c7` matches the chunk text exactly.

**Dense and BM25 ranks for the sanity query.** I embedded the query with the query path used by `knowledge_base.search`, then queried the v9 Qdrant vectors independently. The labeled point ranks **2,387 / 286,723 dense** and **1,204 / 107,201 BM25 matches** globally. Within PMBOK alone it ranks **28 / 523 dense** and **26 / 523 BM25**. Neither leg puts it near the global top 50. Those ranks show weak query-to-passage similarity, rather than a missing or text-corrupted PMBOK point.

**Plain PMBOK controls.** Three no-rerank questions all returned PMBOK passages in their top 10:

- “what are the eight project performance domains” — 7 PMBOK results, at ranks 1–7.
- “how does a project team tailor its development approach and life cycle to uncertainty” — 7 PMBOK results, first at rank 1.
- “what are examples of ways to respond to complexity in a project” — 4 PMBOK results, first at rank 1.

**Named example and query wording.** The query relies on the specific airport retail example, which occurs in the labeled chunk; the shopping-malls/entertainment comparison appears in one child chunk in the local chunk corpus. The query retains “airport” but replaces the passage’s “consumer buying habits” with “retail demand” and “likely customer behavior,” and omits its distinctive hooks: “simulation,” “similar though unrelated scenarios,” and “analogous information” from shopping malls. This wording also resembles retail-demand forecasting, which appears among the competing top fused results. As a control, “In PMBOK, what simulation analogy could help estimate airport shopping and restaurant consumer buying habits using shopping malls?” retrieves `pmbok-guide-7e-p46-c7` at **rank 1** in the unchanged v9 index.

Taken together, PMBOK is indexed correctly; the failed query is a weakly anchored paraphrase for this passage. The label is substantively correct, but the query should retain a source concept such as simulation or analogy if this passage is meant to be retrieved reliably.
